Degree

B.S. Computer Science, Honors

0Expected graduation, May

I'm in the W.A. Franke Honors College at the University of Arizona. Honors standing means smaller honors sections of core courses, a faculty-mentored thesis or capstone before graduation, and priority access to undergraduate research, which is how I landed in an aerospace lab in my first semester.

The computer science track front-loads systems: data structures and algorithms first, then operating systems and distributed systems, with database systems in progress now. That sequence is deliberate. Everything I've shipped at Grab and in my own projects comes back to concurrency, state and storage.

Outside the degree plan I completed the IBM Data Science Professional Certificate on Coursera, a ten-course sequence that covers Python, SQL, analysis, visualisation and machine learning, ending in a capstone.
